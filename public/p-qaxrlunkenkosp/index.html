He coaches everyone from complete beginners to professional athletes. Whoever you are, the aim is the same: a way of training and eating you can keep up for life.

Questions

Do I need to be fit already?

No. Josh coaches complete beginners right up to professional athletes, and your plan starts where you are.

Do I need a gym membership for online coaching?

No. Plans can be built for home, outdoors or a gym, using whatever kit you have.

Will I have to give up foods I like?

The aim is a way of eating you can keep up for good, so your nutrition is built around your life rather than a list of banned foods.

What happens after I message?

Josh will chat through your goals and how you like to train, then build your plan and send it to your JD Fitness app.

Where is the studio?

LS18 Training is in Horsforth, Leeds. Message Josh for directions when you book.
